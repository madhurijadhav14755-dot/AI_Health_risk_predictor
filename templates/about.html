About Our Project

AI Health Risk Predictor

Our AI Health Risk Predictor is a smart web application that analyzes user health data such as age, BMI, glucose level, blood pressure, and heart rate to predict possible health risks using Artificial Intelligence techniques.

Try Prediction

🎯 Our Mission

To provide early health risk prediction so users can take preventive actions before serious health issues occur.

🧠 Technology Used

Django, Python, Machine Learning, HTML, CSS, and modern UI design principles.

⚡ How It Works

Users enter health parameters → AI model analyzes data → system predicts risk level instantly.
